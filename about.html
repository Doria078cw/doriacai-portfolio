Doria Wei Cai

HCI Researcher

Human–AI Co-Creation · Creativity Support · Multimodal & Spatial Interaction

Hi, I’m Doria! I’m an HCI researcher who recently completed my M.A. in Design and Development of Digital Games at Columbia University. I’m interested in human–AI co-creation, creativity support tools, and multimodal interaction. My research explores how AI can better support human creativity, expression, and collaboration.

I have been fortunate to receive research mentorship from Prof. Xiang “Anthony” Chen at UCLA, Prof. Ray LC at City University of Hong Kong, Prof. Joey Lee at Columbia University, and Prof. Lei Zhang at NJIT.

Beyond research, I love making games and experimenting with AI in my own creative work. I won Best Design at Global Game Jam, and my AI short film was selected as a finalist and Autodesk Award nominee at the MIT AI Film Festival. In industry, I have worked on AI-powered creation tools at ByteDance, Meitu, and Kaze.ai, with projects in generative image and video, 3D, AR, and creative AI experiences.
