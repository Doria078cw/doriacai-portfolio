Doodle Spaceship

Learning about planetary environments by assembling and testing spacecraft.

Educational GameInteraction Design2022
BackgroundMechanismInteraction paradigmInterface prototype

My contribution

  • Conducted observations, interviews, and research into children’s astronomy learning.
  • Designed the spacecraft components, game flow, and interface.
  • Built and iterated paper prototypes and high-fidelity screens.

Background

Children’s curiosity about space can be difficult to connect with abstract facts about planets. Observations and interviews informed a game concept that combines hands-on construction with the environmental constraints of a destination.

Mechanism

Players choose a planet, assemble a spacecraft, and test whether it can land. Conditions such as gravity, atmosphere, terrain, and temperature create requirements for the craft, turning planetary information into decisions about which components to use.

Interaction paradigm

The main loop is assemble, test, inspect feedback, and revise. Paper prototyping suggested that children preferred learning through trial and error, but needed clearer component functions. I organized parts by function to make the available choices easier to understand.

Interface prototype

The screens connect a destination map, a component-based assembly workspace, and landing feedback. They show how a child can move between choosing a destination and revising a spacecraft after an unsuccessful attempt.
