Background

Dawning imagines a society where individuality is suppressed and obedience is enforced. Developed with Ruotong Cui and Ruiqi Zhang, the game asks players to navigate the tension between following the rules and escaping the system.

Mechanism

Conformity becomes a rule of play. Players observe a model classroom, adjust a matching classroom, and avoid surveillance before moving into an area where they must imitate other citizens. Repetition and observation turn the theme into concrete actions.

Interaction & consequences

Movement, observation, imitation, and timed decisions drive progression. Later situations complicate obedience: an action permitted by the world can still reduce the player’s remaining escape time. These consequences connect the fictional rules with the player’s goal.
