Overview

ConCreate turns an initial idea into a video by coordinating scriptwriting, voiceover generation, stock-footage selection, and editing. The workflow brings together tasks that would otherwise require creators to move between several tools.

My contribution

  • Designed the user experience and interface.
  • Implemented the frontend in a two-person team.

The experience

Creators begin with an idea and a desired video length, review the script, select a voice, and preview the finished video. The system combines generated narration with relevant stock footage and assembles the result for download.
