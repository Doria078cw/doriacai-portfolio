Overview

Kaze AI brings image and video generation together with conversational editing. Users can create visual content and describe changes through chat, making iteration part of the creative workflow. The product connects generative media capabilities with an interface for developing and refining visual ideas.

My contribution

  • Researched creator workflows through interviews, surveys, and behavioral analysis.
  • Translated findings into video presets and creation workflows.
  • Supported feature iteration through competitive research, A/B experimentation, and UX validation.
