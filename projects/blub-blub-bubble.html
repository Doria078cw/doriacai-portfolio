BlubBlub Bubble

Game · 2025
Play on itch.io ↗

Overview

BlubBlub Bubble is an action game controlled by blowing into a microphone. Players guide a fragile bubble around obstacles and power-ups while keeping it inflated.

My contribution

  • Contributed gameplay programming.
  • Designed the user interface.

Interaction paradigm

Players activate the microphone with the left mouse button, blow to move the bubble, and hover over it to inflate it. Breath and pointer input work together as players avoid obstacles and collect power-ups.
